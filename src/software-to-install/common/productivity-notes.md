# Productivity & Notes

Note-taking, personal knowledge management, reference managers, mind mapping, reading, office suites, and productivity apps.

## Mandatory

- Notion `cask:notion`
  - No official Linux app; install the web app as a PWA from the browser.
- Libre Office `flatpak:org.libreoffice.LibreOffice` `cask:libreoffice`
  - Fedora ships LibreOffice preinstalled.

## Optional

### Notes & knowledge management

- [Boostnote](https://boostnote.io/)
- [Joplin](https://github.com/laurent22/joplin/)
- [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md)
- [Logseq](https://logseq.com)
- [Zettlr](https://www.zettlr.com/)
- [RedNotebook](https://rednotebook.sourceforge.io): desktop journal.

### Reference & mind mapping

- [Zotero](https://www.zotero.org/)
- [Freeplane](https://github.com/freeplane/freeplane). Newer fork of Freemind.

### Reading

- [Readest](https://readest.com) ebook reader. [Code](https://github.com/readest/readest/).

### Productivity

- Pomodoro based productivity app - [Pomotroid](https://github.com/Splode/pomotroid)
- [Kaneo](https://kaneo.app/): self-hosted project management platform (Docker). [Code](https://github.com/usekaneo/kaneo).
